NU:CAMPUS CHAOS
Protect the Campus. Survive the Semester.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Select a dog card at the bottom.
  2. Check its NU Point cost.
  3. Click an empty tile to deploy it (right-click a dog to remove it).
  4. Defend the campus on the left. Each lane has one Teacher waiting at the gate: when an enemy reaches it, the Teacher charges out and clears the whole lane (once per lane).
  5. Defeat incoming Campus Chaos from the right.
  6. Manage your NU Points — Library Dogs make more.
  7. Survive all waves and levels.
  8. Defeat THE FINAL EXAM.
